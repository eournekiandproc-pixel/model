Mount Olympus

The Olympian Gods

According to Greek mythology, these powerful gods lived on Mount Olympus and influenced the world of mortals.

⚡

Zeus

King of the gods and ruler of the sky. His symbols include the thunderbolt, eagle, and oak tree.

Sky • Thunder • Justice
🌊

Poseidon

God of the sea, earthquakes, and horses. He is often shown carrying a three-pronged trident.

Sea • Storms • Horses
🏛️

Athena

Goddess of wisdom, strategy, and crafts. The owl and olive tree are among her symbols.

Wisdom • Strategy
☀️

Apollo

God associated with music, prophecy, healing, and the sun. His symbols include the lyre and laurel.

Music • Prophecy • Light
🌹

Aphrodite

Goddess of love and beauty, often associated with the sea, doves, roses, and myrtle.

Love • Beauty
🔥

Hephaestus

God of fire, metalworking, and craftsmanship. In myths, he created remarkable objects for gods and heroes.

Fire • Craft • Invention

🦁

Known for completing twelve extraordinary labours, including the capture of the Nemean Lion.

🧵

Theseus

Remembered for entering the Labyrinth and defeating the Minotaur with help from Ariadne.

🏹

Odysseus

🛡️

Achilles

Stories through time

One Myth to Begin With

Persephone and the Seasons

In one famous Greek myth, Persephone, daughter of Demeter, spends part of the year in the Underworld and part with her mother. Demeter is the goddess of agriculture, and her grief during Persephone's absence is used to explain the barren months. Her joyful reunion helps bring the earth back to life. The story became an ancient way of understanding the changing seasons.

Myths have many versions, and details can differ between ancient authors.
